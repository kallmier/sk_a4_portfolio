Organisation & Administration

Why This Matters at RespawnRitual Studios

In a game studio environment, organisation is essential because it allows for maximum efficiency, reduced stress when dealing with deadlines and everyday tasks, and improves focus. Without a system in place the work environment can quickly turn to chaos and the efficiency and quality of the work produced drops significantly. As someone who has experience in accounts receivable, I understand the importance of organisation.

My Experiences

In my role as Accounts Officer at my local high school, I was responsible for the weekly bank reconciliation and helping the teachers with processing their receipts to get their money back when they purchased supplies for the school. This involved going through bank statements and allocating the payments received into the correct accounting files to make sure we knew what amount went where. this required me to spend a good amount of time making sure i had all the information I needed before starting the task as if one was missing it would create issues later on.
