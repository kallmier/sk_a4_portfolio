Organisation & Administration

Why This Matters at RespawnRitual Studios

In a game studio environment, organisation is essential because it allows for maximum efficiency, reduced stress when dealing with deadlines and everyday tasks, and improves focus. Without a system in place the work environment can quickly turn to chaos and the efficiency and quality of the work produced drops significantly. As someone who has experience in accounts receivable, I understand the importance of organisation.

My Experiences

As Accounts Officer at a local high school, I was responsible for weekly bank reconciliation and processing staff expense reimbursements for school supply purchases. This involved reviewing bank statements, accurately allocating incoming payments to the correct accounts, and maintaining detailed records throughout. The role demanded careful preparation before beginning each task, any missing information could create discrepancies downstream, reinforcing my approach of verifying all necessary data before proceeding.

Examples

  • Managing my time across multiple tasks in the canteen
  • Keeping accurate records in accounts
  • Consistently met administrative deadlines, including weekly reporting cycles requiring precision and attention to detail
