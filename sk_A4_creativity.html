Creativity

Why This Matters at RespawnRitual Studios

Creativity is at the heart of everything a game studio produces. Without immersive storytelling, visual design and relevant music, a game studio cannot function. As someone who has played their fair share of games, I can bring an objective eye to a creative team.

My Experience

While I have not yet created my own game, I have spent years as an engaged player analysing what makes games compelling. This has given me an eye for not only visual design but also gameplay programming. I notice that the small touches really make the game feel alive, the colour palette can make a game feel dark and gritty, or light and breezy. I apply the same thinking while crocheting, choosing colours that match well together or ones that create contrast.

Examples

  • Crochet - planning a pattern, choosing colours, problem solving when it goes wrong
  • Gaming - analysing what makes a game fun, noticing design choices
  • This portfolio - designing the colour scheme, layout decisions I've made
